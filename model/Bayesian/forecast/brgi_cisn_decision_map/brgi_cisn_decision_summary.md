# BRGI + CISN Decision Map Summary

## Plain-language Interpretation

BRGI identifies where forecasted RMD burden exceeds PT readiness. CISN identifies PTs with broader cross-RMD spillover potential. Together, they convert many forecast curves into an actionable innovation-priority map.

## How to Read the Figure

**Panel A** shows the largest RMD–PT gaps.

**Panel B** shows PTs with broader cross-RMD spillover value.

**Panel C** combines gap urgency and spillover value into four priority groups.

## Priority Categories

**Strategic Priority**: Large gap and broad cross-RMD utility.

**Targeted Priority**: Large gap but mostly disorder-specific value.

**Platform Opportunity**: Broad cross-RMD value but lower immediate gap urgency.

**Watch**: Lower immediate gap urgency and lower spillover value.
