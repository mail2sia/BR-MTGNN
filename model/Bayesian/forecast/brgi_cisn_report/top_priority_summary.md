## Interpretation and Claim Boundaries
**Important:** CISN scores should be interpreted as hypothesis-generating prioritization signals. A high CISN score indicates that a PT may have cross-RMD portfolio relevance because of temporal similarity, disorder similarity, or intervention-transfer evidence. It does NOT establish clinical efficacy, causal transferability, or treatment recommendation validity.

**What BRGI + CISN Tell You:**
- BRGI identifies where forecasted gaps are largest (burden − readiness)
- CISN identifies PTs that may help related RMDs through spillover mechanisms
- Combined, they highlight strategic opportunities for R&D, clinical trials, and portfolio planning

**What BRGI + CISN Do NOT Tell You:**
- ❌ Clinical efficacy (requires randomized trials)
- ❌ Causal mechanism (requires mechanistic research)
- ❌ Implementation feasibility (requires operational analysis)
- ❌ Economic ROI (requires cost-benefit analysis)

**How to Use:**
1. Identify Strategic Priorities for immediate R&D/clinical focus
2. Consider Platform Opportunities for infrastructure investment
3. Use spillover network edges to design multi-disorder trials
4. Validate hypotheses with clinical and mechanistic evidence
